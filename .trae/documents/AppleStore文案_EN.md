# Description

TalkPilot is an AI language conversation assistant built for real-world conversations.

Whether you're traveling abroad, studying overseas, communicating at work, or just chatting with friends in another language, TalkPilot helps you understand the other person and offers natural, practical expression suggestions when you need them.

Turn on real-time conversation assistance with one tap. The app provides translations, reply suggestions, and naturalness tips based on what's being said, so you're not just "translating a sentence" — you're gradually learning to express yourself more naturally in real contexts.

When you get stuck and don't know what to say, switch to SOS mode. Say what you mean in your native language, and TalkPilot instantly turns it into the target language and reads it aloud for the other person to hear — no more awkward silences, keeping the conversation flowing.

After each conversation, TalkPilot generates a review highlighting your best expressions, areas to improve, and more natural ways to phrase things. Every real conversation becomes an effective practice session.

Key Features:

Real-Time Conversation Assistance
